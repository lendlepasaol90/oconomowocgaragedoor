What Counts as Urgent

When a Door Problem Cannot Wait Until Next Week

Some garage door problems are annoying. Others put your home, your car or your schedule at risk. A door stuck in the open position means anyone walking by can see into the garage and, often, reach the door into the house. A door frozen shut with the car inside means you are missing work, school drop-off or an appointment. A door hanging crooked on one cable is a falling hazard for anyone who walks under it.

Those are the calls we move to the front of the line. Our promise is simple and honest: same-day service when you call early. We do not claim around-the-clock service, because a tired technician working a tensioned spring by flashlight at three in the morning is not doing you any favors. What we do is keep the day flexible enough to reach urgent calls quickly and carry the parts that solve most failures in one trip.

If you are unsure whether your situation is an emergency, call and describe it. We will tell you honestly whether it needs a visit today or can safely wait for a regular appointment.

  • Door stuck open and will not close
  • Car trapped inside the garage
  • Broken spring with the door down
  • Door off its track or hanging crooked
  • Snapped or unwound lift cable
  • Door hit by a vehicle

Securing the Home

First We Make It Safe, Then We Make It Right

On an emergency call, the first job is getting the door into a safe, secure position. If a door is jammed open, we stabilize it, determine whether it can be lowered without causing more damage, and close it so your house is protected. If a door is partly off its track, we clamp it in place before anything moves so it cannot swing or drop. If a car needs to get out, we focus on freeing the door and getting it open safely.

Once the door is stable, we go through the system to find what actually failed. In most emergencies it is a broken torsion or extension spring, a cable that has frayed or jumped off its drum, a roller that broke and let the door leave the track, or an opener that lost its connection to the door. We carry springs, cables, rollers, hinges and common opener parts so that the full repair can usually happen on the same visit.

When a part has to be ordered, we do not leave your home exposed. We set the door so it is closed and locked or otherwise secure, explain the plan, and come back when the part arrives.

While You Wait

What to Do Before We Arrive

A few simple steps keep a bad situation from getting worse. Stop pressing the opener button. If a spring has broken or a cable has failed, the opener is trying to lift weight it was never designed to carry, and it can strip its gears or bend the top panel. Unplug the opener if you can reach the plug safely.

Keep children, pets and vehicles away from the door opening. Do not walk under a door that is hanging unevenly. Do not try to loosen, wind, or remove springs, cables or the bottom brackets. Those parts hold a great deal of stored energy and cause serious injuries every year. Leave them for a trained technician.

If the door is stuck open and you are worried about security, close and lock the door between the garage and the house. Move valuables out of sight if you can do so without going under the door. Then call us with a description of what you see and hear. A photo of the spring area or the bottom corners of the door helps us bring the right parts.

What To Look For

Signs You Need Same-Day Service

Get A Free Quote

A Loud Bang From the Garage

A sudden bang, often in the morning, usually means a spring has broken. The door may still appear normal but will not lift, or will lift only a few inches before the opener stops. Do not keep trying the opener. Call for same-day service.

Door Stuck in the Open Position

If the door will not come down, your garage and possibly your house are exposed. The cause might be a sensor issue, a broken part, or a door jammed in its track. We focus first on safely closing and securing it.

Car Trapped in the Garage

When the door will not open and your vehicle is inside, every hour counts. Avoid forcing the door up by hand if it feels very heavy, because that usually means a broken spring. We prioritize freeing the door so you can leave.

Door Hanging at an Angle

A door that is higher on one side has likely lost a cable or slipped a drum. The door can twist out of the track or fall. Keep everyone clear, stop using the opener, and call so we can secure it.

Panel Damage From a Vehicle

A bumped door can bend panels, kink the track and pop rollers loose. Even if it still moves, damaged sections can buckle or bind on the next cycle. We secure the door and assess whether panels can be straightened or need replacing.

Opener Runs but Door Stays

If the motor hums or runs but the door does not move, the drive gear, trolley or door connection may have failed, or the door itself may be too heavy because of a spring problem. We check both before replacing anything.

Common Emergencies

The Failures We See Most Often

Broken springs top the list, and they tend to break on the coldest mornings. The steel is already near the end of its life, cold makes it less forgiving, and the first lift of the day is the one that finishes it. The homeowner hears a loud bang from the garage and finds the opener straining against a door that will not move.

Cable failures are close behind. A cable frayed by rust at the bottom bracket can let go without warning, dropping one side of the door. Vehicle strikes are common too, especially in winter when a car slides on an icy driveway or someone backs out before the door has fully opened. Opener problems round out the list: a stripped drive gear, a broken trolley, or a power surge that leaves the motor unresponsive. Each of these looks similar from inside the garage, which is why we diagnose before we repair.

Local Know-How

Cold Mornings and Snow Days in Oconomowoc

In Oconomowoc, emergency calls follow the weather. When the temperature drops hard, worn springs that held up all fall break on the first lift of the morning. Heavy snow and ice cause another wave: bottom seals freeze to the slab, and people slide into doors on icy driveways along the hills and lake roads around town. Detached garages at older lakefront homes near Lac La Belle and Oconomowoc Lake often sit unheated, so their hardware takes the cold hardest. Commuters heading for I-94 or Highway 16 cannot afford a trapped car, which is why we keep room in the day for urgent calls. If your door is showing signs of wear in late fall, a routine visit before the cold arrives can prevent a winter emergency.

Ask A Technician
01Do you offer 24/7 emergency garage door service?

No. We offer same-day service when you call early, and we keep our schedule flexible for urgent problems like doors stuck open or cars trapped inside. If you call late in the day, we will help you secure the situation over the phone where possible and schedule the earliest available visit, usually first thing the next morning.

02My door is stuck open. What should I do right now?

Stop using the opener and unplug it if you can reach it safely. Keep everyone out from under the door. Lock the door between the garage and the house, and move any valuables out of view if you can do so without passing under the door. Then call us with a description and, if possible, a photo of the springs and bottom corners.

03Can I open the door by hand to get my car out?

Sometimes, but be careful. If the springs are intact, pulling the red emergency release lets you lift the door manually. If a spring is broken, the door will be extremely heavy and can drop without warning. If the door feels much heavier than normal, do not force it. Call us and we will open it safely.

04Will an emergency visit cost more than a regular repair?

We quote the price before work starts on every call, emergency or not, so you know the cost before deciding. The best way to keep costs down is to avoid running the opener against a broken door, because that can damage the opener and panels and turn a simple repair into a larger one.

05Can you fix the door on the first visit?

Usually, yes. We carry springs, cables, rollers, hinges, brackets and common opener parts on the truck. Some doors need a special-order part, such as a specific panel or an unusual spring size. In that case we secure the door so your home is protected and return to finish the repair when the part arrives.

06Which Lake Country towns do you cover for urgent repairs?

We cover Oconomowoc and nearby communities, including the Village of Lac La Belle, Village of Oconomowoc Lake, Town of Oconomowoc, Summit, Okauchee, Delafield, Nashotah, Hartland, Dousman, Ixonia, Merton, Chenequa, Watertown and Ashippun. Calling early in the day gives us the best chance of reaching any of these areas the same day.
